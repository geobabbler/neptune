## Three Topics That Stood Out

**1. Two international standards bodies get new leadership in the same week**

Cesium founder and Bentley Systems Chief Platform Officer Patrick Cozzi was elected to the OGC Board of Directors, per the Open Geospatial Consortium. Separately, New Zealand's Adam Greenland began his term as a Director with the International Hydrographic Organization, Spatial Source reports.

*Why this matters:* Open standards work rarely makes headlines on its own, but who sits on these boards shapes how 3D data, digital twins and hydrographic sovereignty questions get resolved. A Cesium founder moving into OGC governance is a concrete link between the 3D geospatial world and the standards that underpin it.

**2. Billions in EO procurement collide with a reality check on what satellites can see**

TerraWatch's Earth Observation Essentials roundup covers NOAA's selection of 14 companies to compete for a 10-year, $2.35 billion commercial weather satellite data contract, plus Tomorrow.io's plans for a five-instrument DeepSky constellation. The same week, an explainer on Medium pushes back on pop-culture expectations of satellite surveillance, walking through what current commercial imagery actually resolves and what it doesn't.

*Why this matters:* Landscape context flags commercial EO constellation economics as one of the ecosystem's biggest coverage gaps. TerraWatch fills part of that gap with a real number attached, and pairing it with a grounded look at imagery limits is a useful check on how much capability that money is actually buying.

**3. Esri's GeoAI tooling keeps showing up in land administration work**

A new book from CSDILA researchers, "Land Intelligence and Geospatial Ecosystem," covered by Spatial Source, lays out how GeoAI, digital twins and BIM are reshaping land administration and the wider geospatial ecosystem. On the tooling side, Esri's ArcGIS Blog published a guide to working embeddings into spatial analysis workflows in ArcGIS Pro.

*Why this matters:* Landscape context notes the Earth embeddings sub-theme went quiet after April's standards sprints. This isn't that same conversation, but it shows embeddings moving from research and standards talk into a shipped feature in a mainstream GIS platform, exactly the kind of applied step that thread was waiting on.
