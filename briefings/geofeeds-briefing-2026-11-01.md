A recap of CNG Zürich, which was booked out. The phrase heard most often was "files in a bucket," and the post traces how far that idea carries, from PMTiles and Portolan to GeoZarr and GeoEmbeddings. It's a quick read on where the cloud-native crowd thinks the format work is heading.
→ [Files in a bucket: CNG Zürich](https://spatialists.ch/posts/2026/11/01-files-in-a-bucket-cng-zurich/)

**2. The finding that shows up in nearly every ArcGIS security review** — *Spatial Source*
The answer is shared administrative accounts, which the authors say are among the most common findings and also among the most often deferred. They argue the fix is simpler than people assume, since shared accounts weaken attribution, accountability and least privilege. Rare practical security advice for ArcGIS administrators.
→ [The finding that shows up in nearly every ArcGIS security review](https://www.spatialsource.com.au/the-finding-that-shows-up-in-nearly-every-arcgis-security-review/)
