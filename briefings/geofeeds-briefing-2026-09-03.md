*Why this matters:* The feeds mostly cover North America and Europe. **2. Point cloud infrastructure moves on two fronts: a new standard and new hardware**

OGC published the LAZ 1.4 Community Standard, a lossless compression format for LAS 1.4 point cloud data, formalizing how the industry stores and shares lidar scans. Hours later, NavVis launched its CLX dual-lidar scanner for as-built documentation and reality capture, covered independently by both Spatial Source and Earth Imaging Journal. A standards body and a hardware vendor moved on point clouds the same day.

*Why this matters:* Point cloud and lidar workflows are one of the most conspicuously underserved topics in these feeds despite massive market growth. A new compression standard landing alongside new capture hardware is a real signal this corner of the industry is maturing, not just a coincidence worth ignoring.
