## Three Topics That Stood Out

**1. Basemaps and EO platforms keep shipping, one launch at a time**

Indonesia stood up Piksel, a national open platform for exploring and analysing analysis-ready Earth observation data, as reported by Spatial Source. The same cycle, Esri and Airbus expanded World Imagery with new high-resolution basemap coverage across 2,000 cities worldwide, part of a broader push announced at the 2026 Esri User Conference. In the UK, PlanningMapsUK launched an online Ordnance Survey mapping platform letting homeowners, architects and property professionals generate licensed site plans and CAD mapping without leaving the browser.

*Why this matters:* A national government, a basemap duopoly and a planning startup all shipped mapping infrastructure the same week. Analysis-ready imagery and basemap access keep getting commoditized, which raises the bar for what counts as a differentiator in this space.

**2. The vendors keep narrating the customers who don't write**

EarthDaily expanded its AI-powered wildfire risk model, FLAIM, across the western US in partnership with a named North American insurance major, a rare direct look at how insurers actually buy EO-derived risk intelligence. Teledyne FLIR OEM was selected for a US Army DUTCH award to advance next-generation uncooled thermal imaging, framed around strengthening the domestic defense industrial base. FlyGuys, meanwhile, rebranded its internal software as FlyGuys Capture, positioning itself as a standalone reality-data company.

*Why this matters:* Insurance and defense are two of the ecosystem's most persistently underrepresented customer segments, and today both show up only through the vendor's own press release. The buyers still aren't the ones telling the story.

**3. Spatial Source's Australia keeps surveying and staffing itself**

Aerial imagery surveys across three Great Barrier Reef sites captured new data for long-term monitoring, while the Murray-Darling Basin Authority announced it will update a 2003 survey of Menindee Lakes to get current capacity figures. The NSW agency DCS Spatial Services opened a feedback call on its Collaboration Portal and future imagery and elevation products, and Western Australia opened its 2027 Digital Public Sector Graduate Program to GIS graduates.

*Why this matters:* Government remains the most narrated customer segment in these feeds, and Spatial Source is one of the few consistently substantive non-North American voices covering it. Four stories, one government, one news cycle: surveying, consulting and hiring all at once.
