## Quiet Day Across the Feeds

Quiet day across the feeds — here are the highlights. The one real thread: capital and physical infrastructure are moving together in commercial space/EO. TerraWatch's Earth Observation Essentials reports EO satellite manufacturer Muon Space closing a $250M Series C (total raised now over $386M) alongside orbital-data-center startup Starcloud's $250M funding extension, with Nvidia joining this round. The same morning, Geoconnexion covered Leaf Space opening a new ground station in Nova Scotia — the downlink capacity that satellite and orbital-compute buildouts eventually need.

*Why this matters:* Ground segment capacity is the unglamorous bottleneck behind every EO and orbital-compute funding round. Seeing downlink infrastructure and satellite/compute capital show up the same week suggests the buildout is becoming operational rather than aspirational — worth watching whether more ground-station announcements follow the funding.
