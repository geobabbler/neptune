## Quiet Day Across the Feeds

Quiet day across the feeds. Here are the highlights.

**Two acquisitions land on the same day**

Earth Imaging Journal ran Hexagon's agreement to acquire geotechnical software provider Rocscience from TA Associates and its founders, and Intermap's completed purchase of PCI Geomatics. Intermap says the deal doubles its recurring revenue and pulls PCI's analytics into distributed edge processing and microservices.

*Why this matters:* Consolidation keeps rolling. Hexagon is pairing monitoring with engineering simulation, a rare move toward commercial verticals like mining and civil work. Intermap is a small player buying legacy remote sensing software to get scale and recurring revenue.
