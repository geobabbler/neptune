**3. Contrasting Political Maps of Population Density in Eastern Asia** — *GeoCurrents*
It argues that comparing China with its neighbors can mislead because most Chinese province-level divisions are similar in scale to whole neighboring countries. A good reminder that choropleth results depend on how the units are drawn.
→ [Contrasting Political Maps of Population Density in Eastern Asia](https://www.geocurrents.info/blog/2026/11/07/contrasting-political-maps-of-population-density-in-eastern-asia/)

**4. Utopian London** — *Maps Mania*
