**2. Rain from Orbit, Part 3: The Signal Eats Itself** — *Earth Observation on Medium*
This is the third installment in an illustrated series on spaceborne rain radar, following Part 2 on the two boxes of rain a radar can't tell apart. It explains attenuation, why the classical correction blows up exactly when rain is heaviest, and how GPM gets around the problem by measuring the ground. It's a rare technical explainer on sensor physics, a topic the feeds seldom cover in depth.
